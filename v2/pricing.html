Questions doctors ask

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel within your 14-day pilot and we refund the setup fee in full. After that, a monthly plan stops after 30 days' notice, and a yearly plan runs until the year ends.

Who can see my patients' records?

Your clinic's staff, and each person sees only the screens their role needs. The Meddy team opens a record only when you ask us for help with it.

Do patients message my clinic's own WhatsApp number?

Yes, once we connect your number. Until then, bookings run on a Meddy number that greets patients in your clinic's name.

We have two doctors. Do we pay twice?

No. Each plan covers one clinic with up to two doctors. Each extra doctor adds ₹999 a month. With six or more doctors, talk to us about Meddy Custom.

What happens to my data if I leave?

We send you your patient list and visit records, then delete your clinic's data within 30 days of your request.

How long does setup take?

About a week. You share your hours, doctors and prices, and we set up WhatsApp and Clinic OS and train your staff at the clinic.

Prices per clinic in Indian rupees, plus GST. Cancel with 30 days' notice.
