Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · Applies to meddy.co.in

In short
  • We collect only what we need to prepare your Clinic Spotlight and talk to you about it.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

Meddy is a product of Viyuha Technologies, a sole proprietorship based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India ("we", "us"). For this website, we decide how and why your personal data is used, so we are the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

2. What we collect

WhenWhatWhy
You request a Clinic SpotlightClinic name, area and city, WhatsApp number, and your consent (with date and time)To prepare your Spotlight and contact you about it
You email usYour email address and what you writeTo reply to you
You visit the siteBasic technical data your browser sends (such as IP address and browser type), kept in server logsTo keep the site secure and working

To prepare a Spotlight, we also look at information your clinic has already made public: its Google Business Profile, reviews, ratings, website and search rankings, and those of nearby clinics. This is business information, not personal health data.

We do not ask for or collect any patient health information through this website. Please don't send us any.

3. Our legal basis

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the request form. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us (see section 9). Withdrawing consent does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

4. How we use it

  • To prepare and share your Clinic Spotlight.
  • To contact you on WhatsApp, phone or email about your Spotlight and, if you are interested, about Meddy's services.
  • To keep the website secure and fix problems.

We will not send you unrelated marketing, and we will stop contacting you if you ask.

5. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run Meddy, and only as much as they need:

  • Cloud hosting and database providers (for example, Vercel, Railway and Supabase), which store data for us.
  • Google, when we look up your clinic's public listing and website performance.
  • Messaging providers, when we contact you on WhatsApp.

Some of these providers may store data outside India. Where they do, we rely on their security commitments and on transfers permitted under Indian law. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep Spotlight request details for as long as we are in conversation with you, and for up to 12 months after our last contact, unless you become a client (in which case your client agreement applies) or you ask us to delete them sooner.

7. How we protect it

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls so that only our team can see your details, and providers with recognised security practices. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your data, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you can:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Ask us to correct, complete or update it.
  • Ask us to erase it.
  • Withdraw your consent.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied.

10. Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It remembers small preferences in your own browser (such as whether sound is muted), which never leave your device.

11. Children

This website is meant for clinic owners and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will tell people who have given us their details.
